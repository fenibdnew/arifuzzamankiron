About

Arifuzzaman, widely known as Arifuzzaman Kiron or simply Kiron, is a computer hardware specialist, tech entrepreneur, and digital creator based in Feni, Bangladesh. He is the Director of STC Tech Hand, located at Chowdhury Mobile Market in Mohipal, Feni.

Working in the computer servicing industry since 2017, he specializes in laptop motherboard chip-level repair, desktop diagnostics, BIOS programming, custom gaming PC builds, CCTV installation, and IT solutions. Alongside hardware engineering, he develops independent web platforms and contributes digital visual assets to Adobe Stock. He is currently pursuing his BA/BSS degree at Bangladesh Open University.
